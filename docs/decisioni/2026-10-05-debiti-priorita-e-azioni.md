# 2026-10-05 — Debiti (Debts) redesigned: "Priorità e azioni", Simulator inside the loans

- **Choice**: among three prototypes (A list and detail, B chart over time, C priorities and actions) Daniele chose C. Prototypes and comparison in `/mnt/project-files/debiti/proposte/` of the Claude project.
- **Overview** (`/debiti`, no more tabs): upcoming instalments with "Segna pagata" ("Mark as paid") without opening the debt, "Dove paghi di più" ("Where you pay the most": one year's interest at today's balance and rate, per debt), "Come uscirne prima" ("How to get out sooner": extra-per-month slider, avalanche, step-by-step path with a free date and interest saved) and the list of debts.
- **The Simulator is no longer a page**: `/debiti/simulatore` redirects to `/debiti/finanziamenti`. Refinancing (surroga), monthly extra and early repayment live in "E se…" ("What if…") in the loan detail; the avalanche in the overview. The snowball is no longer exposed (in typical cases it matches the avalanche); the `simulatePayoff` engine still supports it.
- **Detail** (`/debiti/finanziamenti?id=`): progress ring with remaining balance and key figures; without `id` the first one opens.
- New Umami events: `debt_simulation_opened` (`kind`: extra, estinzione, surroga) and `debt_exit_plan_used`.
- Postponed: comparison of strategies (avalanche/snowball) in the overview, instalment-by-instalment plan read year by year (option B) and chart of the remaining balance over time. The demo seed now has three loans (previously one).
- **Lombard credit removed (2026-10-06, on request)**: removed UI, calculations, API (`kind: "credit_line"`, events `draw`/`repay`/`interest_charged`), validation, catalog, landing and demo seed. The schema does not change (no migration): the columns and the enum remain and any `credit_line` rows in production are ignored by `buildDebtsView`, so inert and not deleted. A DROP of the columns/rows will be done with a separate migration only if requested.
