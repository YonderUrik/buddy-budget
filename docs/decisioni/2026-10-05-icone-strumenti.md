# 2026-10-05 — Instrument icons in Posizioni (Positions)

- **Choice**: no external logo services (Clearbit is closed, Logo.dev/Brandfetch require a key, attribution and hotlinking). Everything is resolved locally from type and name (`lib/investments/instrument-icon.ts`), without third-party requests from the user's browser.
- **Crypto**: CC0 icons from `cryptocurrency-icons` copied into `public/instrument-icons/crypto/` (~480 coins), matched by name or symbol.
- **Stocks**: CC0 marks from Simple Icons for ~40 well-known companies (list in `scripts/generate-instrument-icons.mjs`); the others show an abbreviation. Low coverage: the big Italian/European names are not in Simple Icons.
- **ETFs and funds**: the issuer is recognized from the name (iShares, Vanguard, Xtrackers…), but instead of the logo there is a coloured abbreviation: they are registered trademarks and we have no permission to use them. For the real logos it is enough to add them to `public/instrument-icons/issuers/` once use is authorized.
- **Logo.dev (added the same day)**: with `LOGODEV_PUBLISHABLE_KEY` the server downloads from `img.logo.dev` and keeps on Redis (30 days, misses 3 days, 5-minute pause after an error) the logo of stocks (by ISIN) and ETFs/funds (by **issuer**, not by ISIN). Reason: a test on 18 ISINs found 17, but one Xtrackers ISIN returned the iShares logo and with `fallback=404` it cannot be detected; asking for the logo of the issuer recognized from the name, the error cannot happen. The browser only requests `/api/instruments/[id]/logo` (404 = the abbreviation stays). Without a key nothing changes. The attribution "Loghi forniti da Logo.dev" ("Logos provided by Logo.dev") appears below the positions only when a remote logo has been shown.
- **To do live**: add `LOGODEV_PUBLISHABLE_KEY` (public key `pk_…`, not the secret one) as a secret in the infra repo; clarify with support@logo.dev the commercial use of the free plan and the issuers' trademarks before enabling it in production.
- **Where they appear**: Posizioni, list and header of Titoli (Securities), pairs and chips of Sovrapposizioni (Overlaps) (`InstrumentLabel`), data sources of Diversificazione, Proventi (Income), tax rates, operations (from `sm` up). Three sizes (`xs` 20 px, `sm` 28 px, `md` 36 px). Also in search results (for market results only the local icon: they do not have an id yet, the remote logo arrives after adding them). Not in the import dialog.
- **Postponed**: icons in the import, alert on the error rate of the logo service, Grafana panel.
- `proxy.ts` excludes `instrument-icons/` from the session check, like `brand/`.
