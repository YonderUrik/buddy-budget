# Panoramica (Overview) with a voice (direction A, revised)

Date: 2026-10-06

Direction A chosen among 4 proposals (mockups in `/mnt/project-files/panoramica/`; B and D remain ideas for later). After the first version with equal tiles, Daniele found it lacked identity: the Panoramica now opens with a greeting and 2-3 computed sentences (month versus usual, next instalment within 14 days, transactions to categorize), the net worth chart remains the only boxed panel, and the other parts are open sections on the page (month with cumulative curve versus usual, «Da sistemare» ("To fix"), upcoming instalments as an agenda, investments with paid-in/gain bar), each with an icon in the colour of its area.
Accessibility: minimum text 14 px, 44 px targets, charts with `role="img"` and description, curve animated only without «riduci movimento» (reduce motion), +/− signs and icons besides colour. New Umami event `overview_tile_clicked`. Removed the three-figure «Questo mese» ("This month") card. No migration. Landing screenshots to be regenerated by the bot after the merge.

Inspiration from Wealthfolio (Daniele's screenshots, only cues of form, not its colours): full-width chart with a single line and gradient, amount with dimmed decimals, period selector centred below the chart, soft rows for asset classes, two columns. The chart has two views: «Totale» ("Total", default) and «Per classe» ("By class": the stacked areas with button legend, unchanged).

Second revision: «Dove sta il patrimonio» ("Where your net worth is") is a donut chart (invested share in the centre, soft rows beside it; debts stay out of the pie). «Da sistemare» was removed entirely from the Panoramica at Daniele's request (card, sentence in the greeting, related Umami events); the notice in the sidebar and the `/categorizza` page remain.
