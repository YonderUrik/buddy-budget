# 2026-10-08 — Investimenti (Investments): period gain and understandable reinvestment switches

- **Bug**: in the Portfolio the gain («il mercato ha aggiunto…», "the market added…") was always the one since inception, even when changing period. Now, for every period other than «Max», the sentence shows the **gain in the period**: change in value net of what you paid in and withdrew in the period, income included (`lib/investments/period-gain.ts`, same basis as Performance's daily returns). With «Max» the current reading remains (paid in / added by the market / already received).
- **Definition**: `valore finale − valore alla chiusura del giorno prima del periodo − (acquisti − vendite e rimborsi) + dividendi e cedole` (final value − value at close of the day before the period − (purchases − sales and redemptions) + dividends and coupons); the percentage is the period return independent of contributions (TWR). It also applies to «Da inizio anno» ("Year to date") and «Personalizzato» ("Custom").
- **Switches**: «Reinvesti costi/imposte» become two rows «Rimetti i costi/le imposte nel portafoglio» ("Put the costs/taxes back into the portfolio") showing, always visible, how much you paid and how much more you would be worth today; when on they show the effect on the total, and the chart puts the «Valore registrato» ("Recorded value") next to the simulated value. Calculation logic unchanged.
- **Observability**: Umami event `investment_reinvest_toggled` (type and state, no amount); no new route or server log.
- **Postponed**: with the simulation active the gain sentence stays hidden (as before); Performance's returns for «Da inizio anno»/«Personalizzato» still use «Max» (out of scope).
- **Brokers and cash**: the «Broker inclusi nel portafoglio» ("Brokers included in the portfolio") block becomes a compact chip in the header («Tutti i broker» / «Broker: 2 di 3») with the list in a popover; same filter logic. The «Includi liquidità» ("Include cash") toggle is removed: it only changed the big figure, while chart, returns and gain are already securities-only. Now the figure is always securities-only and a row below shows the cash at the brokers and the total including it. No calculation or saved preference depended on the toggle (it was only local page state).
