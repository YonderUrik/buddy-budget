# 2026-10-08 — Liquidità (Cash): Conti and Movimenti in a single section

- **Decision**: Conti (Accounts) and Movimenti (Transactions) become **Liquidità** (`/liquidita`, tabs Movimenti · Analisi · Conti · Categorie e regole). Direction chosen among three mockups: «Un flusso unico» ("A single flow": balance and chart at the top, account chips to filter, feed of transactions by day with the month alongside).
- **Style**: that of the Panoramica (computed sentence, figure with dimmed decimals, full-width chart without a card, soft rows, sections with tinted icon) becomes the app standard for new or revised screens.
- **Dividi** ("Split") stays on the existing model (`excludedAmount`, no migration): it now has shortcuts Tutta/Metà/Un terzo/Un quarto (All/Half/A third/A quarter), amount and slider, with the result spelled out. **Category**: changed by tapping the transaction's icon, with «Ricordala» ("Remember it": creates the rule) and undo.
- **Postponed**: Categorie and Regole reuse the existing board and manager inside the new wrapper (dedicated redesign to do); the transaction/note edit forms and the new-account and account-detail windows are the previous ones; «Dividi» across several categories and the reasons for the split would require a model change.
- **Compatibility**: permanent redirects from `/movimenti*`, `/conti` and the old routes; the saved start page (`/conti`, `/movimenti`) is mapped to the new one; renewal links in emails and GoCardless callbacks point to `/liquidita/conti`.
