# Analitiche (Analytics): «e se…» ("what if…") sliders instead of narrative only (2026-10-09)

After the restyling of Panoramica, Liquidità and Pensione, Daniele asked for 3 proposals for Analitiche (mockups in `analitiche-stile/`, the project folder) and chose **C, «Strumento»** ("Tool").

- **What changes**: at the top, the year in which you can stop working (large, with the difference from the saved assumptions), then four sliders (savings, spending, real return, withdrawal rate) that try out the assumptions **without saving them**; below, four answer cards (Strada fatta, Arrivo, Regge, Costi — "Distance covered, Arrival, Holds up, Costs") that change the full-width chart. «Salva queste ipotesi» ("Save these assumptions") makes them final, «Ripristina» ("Reset") discards them. The other assumptions are in the «Tutte le ipotesi» ("All assumptions") dialog (the old panel, unchanged). No more index of questions or assumptions box at the top.
- **Style**: the metrics and the «Per esperti» ("For experts") blocks lose the grey boxes (rule above, large number), as in the Panoramica standard.
- **Calculation**: `lib/analitiche/scenario.ts` (pure, tested): slider bounds and years to the goal with a single assumption changed. The Monte Carlo simulation follows the sliders with `useDeferredValue` so as not to block dragging.
- **Events** (Umami): `analytics_scenario_changed` (field, when the slider is released), `analytics_scenario_reset`, `analytics_scenario_saved` (fields), `analytics_view_selected` (view); `analytics_question_nav` removed. No migration, no new server log/metric.
- **Postponed**: the «Per esperti» details (chart cards of the old six analyses) remain as before, except for the boxes; «Rischio» ("Risk") as its own answer; sliders for volatility and years in retirement; sliders that start from an unknown figure (they stay disabled until spending or savings is known).
