# 2026-10-09 — Liquidità (Cash): add flows (account, bank, transaction, file)

- **Nuovo conto** ("New account") is a two-level dialog: first the choice (Collega la tua banca · Conto manuale · Importa un file CSV o Excel — "Connect your bank · Manual account · Import a CSV or Excel file"), then the chosen step, with «Cambia modo» ("Change method") always available. It replaces the two tabs «Manuale / Collega banca». Renewing an expired consent opens the bank step directly.
- **Collega banca** ("Connect bank"): real labels on the fields, banks with the history they offer, loading skeleton, error with «Riprova» ("Retry"), «nessun risultato» ("no results") with the way out to a manual account, «Ti porto sul sito di …» ("Taking you to …'s site") state during the redirect. A sentence on the read-only nature of the connection.
- **Nuovo movimento** ("New transaction"): type and amount at the top, errors next to the field (and focus on the first error), «Oggi/Ieri» ("Today/Yesterday"), «Aggiungi e inserisci un altro» ("Add and enter another"), and without manual accounts the prompt to create one.
- **Importa da CSV o Excel** ("Import from CSV or Excel", `/importazioni`): item in the «Aggiungi» ("Add") menu and in the new-account choice; page in three steps (where it comes from, the file, the consent). It now also accepts `.xlsx` (converted to CSV in the browser with the same reader as Pensione and Investimenti: the server always receives CSV, no API change). The consent text is the same, just split up (short sentence + «Cosa succede ai tuoi dati», "What happens to your data").
- **Accessibility and mobile**: full-screen dialog below `sm` with a «Chiudi» ("Close") button, 44px-tall fields on phone, linked `label`s (`htmlFor`), errors `role="alert"`, `aria-invalid`/`aria-describedby`, states `role="status"`.
- New Umami event: `account_add_path_chosen` (`banca` | `manuale` | `importa`). No landing impact (no new feature).
- Postponed: direct import of a bank statement into an existing manual account (today the import creates its own account), file preview before sending, redesign of Categorie/Regole.
