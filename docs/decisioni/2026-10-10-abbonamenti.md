# 2026-10-10 — Abbonamenti (Subscriptions): detected from transactions, confirmed by you

Daniele wanted to know for sure which subscriptions he has, starting from the transactions: the **Liquidità › Abbonamenti** tab is born (`/liquidita/abbonamenti`, default chosen: it lives in Liquidità because the data are the transactions; no new sidebar item).
- **Deterministic and explainable detection, without AI** (`lib/calc/subscriptions.ts`): same merchant (`subscriptionKey`), interval between charges compatible with weekly/monthly/quarterly/half-yearly/yearly, stable day of the month, constant amount or with a single price change. A series with varying amounts (metered bill, supermarket, restaurant) does not pass. If a merchant bills more than one (Apple, Amazon) they are separated by amount equal to the cent. Each proposal carries its reasons, shown in the detail.
- **Detection is not saved**: it is recomputed on every request from the outflows of the last 26 months. The `subscriptions` table (migration 0027) holds only the user's choices (confirmed, excluded, ended) and subscriptions added by hand, attached to transactions by the same key. Outflows of the «Te futuro» ("Future you": savings, investments) group never enter detection.
- Totals (month and year) only on **confirmed and active** ones; detected but undecided ones are summed separately («altri N da confermare», "N more to confirm"). «Non addebitati da un po'» ("Not charged for a while") lists those skipped beyond the tolerance (ended or forgotten?), price increases are a section of their own.
- Postponed: link with Debiti instalments (today the mortgage appears and is excluded with «Non lo è», "It isn't"), upcoming charges in the sidebar's «Prossime scadenze» module, alerts (email/push) for increases and forgotten subscriptions, detection on income, recognizing a price change inside a multi-subscription merchant, Grafana panel (metrics already exposed).
