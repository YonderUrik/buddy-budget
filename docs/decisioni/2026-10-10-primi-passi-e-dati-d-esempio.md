# Primi passi ("First steps") and sample data (2026-10-10)

Decision: after onboarding, whoever has no linked bank finds in Panoramica the «Primi passi» checklist (account, transactions, investment, goal) and can start «Esplora con dati d'esempio» ("Explore with sample data").
The checklist fills itself from real data (`lib/start/status.ts`): non-demo account, `auto` transactions on non-demo accounts, investment operations, budgets or saved Analitiche assumptions. It closes with the X (`auth_user.start_checklist_dismissed_at`) and disappears when complete.
The sample data are two accounts with `accounts.is_demo` and six months of transactions; they start only if the user has no accounts, a strip on every screen marks them as demo and resets them. So as not to mix them with real data, while they are active creating accounts, transactions or bank connections responds 409 `demo_active` (`rejectIfDemoActive`). The reset removes demo accounts, transactions and cash history.
Observability: Umami events `start_*` and `demo_*`, logs `onboarding.*`, metrics `users_activation{step}` and `start_events_total{event}`; existing metrics on accounts and transactions exclude demo data. Migration 0026 (0025 belongs to the summary-emails PR, opened earlier).
No email or reminder: summary and alert emails are a separate piece of work, with unsubscribe and notification preferences.
Postponed: reopening the dismissed checklist (the route supports it, the UI entry point is missing), demo with investments and budgets, 409 guard also on custom imports and investment operations, export that skips demo accounts, Analitiche empty state.
