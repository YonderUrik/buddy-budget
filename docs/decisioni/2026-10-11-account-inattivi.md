# Inactive accounts: notice at 83 days, deletion at 90 (2026-10-11)

Decision: accounts without sign-ins for 90 days are deleted, with a warning email 7 days earlier. Cron `/api/cron/inactive-accounts` (CronJob in the infra repo, every day at 03:15 UTC), `INACTIVITY_CLEANUP_MODE` = `off` | `dry-run` (default) | `execute`: deletion is irreversible, so it is enabled by hand in the Secret after a few days of dry-run (runbook `account-inattivi.md` in the infra repo).
«Inactive»: no sign-in. What counts is the most recent between `auth_user.last_active_at` (updated by login and session renewal, better-auth hook in `lib/auth/index.ts`, migration 0029) and the last session. Automatic bank syncs do not count: the GoCardless consent lasts at most 90 days anyway.
Flow: notice at 83 days (`inactivity_warned_at`, only once; it is reserved before sending and released if sending fails), deletion only if the notice went out at least 7 days earlier and there has been no sign-in since. Never deletion without notice, even if the cron is stalled: the notice goes out late and the deletion 7 days later. A sign-in clears the notice immediately. Deletion is `deleteUserAccount` (GoCardless revocation, all tables, Redis); only backups (14 days) and logs with the hash (30 days) remain, stated in the email and in the Privacy policy.
Choice compared with the request «ogni mese» ("every month"): the cron runs every day, because the 7-day notice cannot be guaranteed with a monthly run (it costs a light query). Safeguards: cap of 50 notices and 50 deletions per run, re-reading the user right before acting, accounts with a deactivation already scheduled excluded, extension point `exemptionReason` for paid subscriptions when they exist (today no exception).
Privacy and Terms updated (retention, «Sospensione e chiusura», "Suspension and closure"); `LEGAL_VERSION` moved to 2026-10-12: **every user must accept again** and the texts must be reviewed by a professional (the closure clause is among those requiring specific approval).
Postponed: cron in the self-hosting compose (whoever hosts decides whether to delete their own users), old unverified emails, expired GoCardless connections and orphaned data (evaluated, see the PR), exception for subscriptions when they arrive.
