# Categorization with an LLM, Phase 1: proposals and cards

- **Decision (2026-10-11)**: after every GoCardless sync and every import, if the user has consented (Impostazioni → Assistente per le categorie, "Settings → Category assistant", off by default), Jev proposes a category for each merchant still to be categorized. Proposals live in `categorization_suggestions` (migration 0028): they are not rules and never categorize on their own; they serve to avoid calling the model again for the same merchant.
- **Privacy**: only the cleaned merchant name is sent (filter `prepareMerchantForModel`: no bank transfers, people, amounts, dates), with ZDR. Legal text added in `landing/content/legal.ts` **without** raising `LEGAL_VERSION`: to be reviewed and then decide whether to ask for a new acceptance.
- **Interface**: `/categorizza` has the «Una alla volta» ("One at a time") view (cards: confirm, change, skip, undo; gesture, buttons, arrows and Z) and the «Elenco» ("List") view. Choices are saved in bulk after a 5-second pause or on leaving, so they can be undone until then. No rule is created unless «Ricorda per questo esercente» is ticked; the list no longer creates rules on its own either.
- **Postponed**: web search, deletion of past rules (Phase 6), cleanup of Jaccard/Ollama, category descriptions for Jev.
- **Landing**: no impact (feature behind consent, catalog to be updated when it is active for everyone).
- **Update (same day)**: consent to the assistant is asked during onboarding (dedicated step with explanation, on by default, shown only if the service is configured); already registered users stay off until they enable it in Impostazioni. The cards also have the upward gesture to skip, a light vibration on a successful gesture and an explanation on first opening (reopenable with «Come funziona», "How it works"; remembered in localStorage).
- **Update 2**: «Categorizza con l'AI» ("Categorize with AI") banner reduced to one line; prompt also in the Movimenti toolbar; the guide dialog is replaced by an animated five-step tour (it opens the first time, «Come funziona» reopens it); added the draft of the announcement email (`featureAnnouncementEmail`, image and steps in the template), **not yet sent**: it needs the controller's approval and a choice on legal basis and unsubscribe (today there is no «novità», "news", preference). Generation for existing transactions runs in `after()` without state on Redis (exception to the long-job standard: proposals are unique by constraint, a double click only costs one extra call).
