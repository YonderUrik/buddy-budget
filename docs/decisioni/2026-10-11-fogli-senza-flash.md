# Bottom sheets without flash (2026-10-11)

On phone, when closing a sheet (Liquidità views, dialogs, confirmations, popovers) the dark backdrop went dark again for an instant: its fade (100 ms) ended before the sheet's slide (200 ms) and without `fill-mode: forwards` it returned to its full state until the sheet was removed. Now backdrop and sheet share duration and curve (`SHEET_MOTION_CLASS` in `components/ui/sheet-handle.tsx`: 280 ms in with the iOS sheet curve, 200 ms out), stay on the final state until removal, the sheet slides without fading and the backdrop blur remains only from `sm` up. Desktop unchanged.
