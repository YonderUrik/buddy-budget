# 2026-10-11 — «Aliquote degli strumenti» ("Instrument tax rates") list only with securities in the portfolio

The list in the Tasse (Taxes) tab showed all instruments loaded for the user: also securities sold in past years and those present only in the target allocation. They were the user's own data (the queries are already filtered by user), but they looked like foreign securities. Now the list shows only open positions and securities with a sale, a redemption or income in the current year (`rateListInstruments` in `lib/investments/tax-view.ts`). The tax calculation and the rest of the tab do not change. Test with two users in `lib/investments/tax-list-isolation.integration.test.ts`.
