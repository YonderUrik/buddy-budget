# Notifications in onboarding and summary with investments (2026-10-11)

- **Onboarding**: new step «Quali email vuoi ricevere?» ("Which emails do you want to receive?") before the confirmations, with the same preferences as Impostazioni → Notifiche (`notification_preferences`, no migration). Everything stays off until the user turns it on (opt-in); the preview box shows the emails that will arrive. Animations from `motion` with the shared springs, no light effects, reduced motion respected.
- **Summary**: one sentence with spent, received and investment gain, then two short groups. Liquidità: income, expenses, set aside, comparison with the previous period, heaviest item, budgets exceeded. Investimenti: value at end of period, period gain (TWR in parentheses), dividends and coupons, invested or withdrawn. The figures come from `computePeriodGain` (the same engine as the Investimenti page) cut to the summary period. Data only, no advice.
- The summary goes out even without transactions if there are investments; if the investment calculation fails it goes out with cash only (log `notifications.digest.investments_failed`, metric `buddybudget_notification_digest_sections_total`).
- Postponed: net worth and debts in the summary, user choice of sections, test on real email clients.
