# 2026-10-11 — Tax regime: from a section to a row at the bottom of the Tasse (Taxes) tab

- **Request**: Daniele found the «Regime fiscale» ("Tax regime") section useless because it seemed not to change anything.
- **Check**: the regime is used by `computeTaxReport` (offsetting of minusvalenze, capital losses, event by event under the regime amministrato — administered regime, the broker withholds tax —, annual net under the regime dichiarativo — declarative regime, the investor declares and pays —; the moment when prior losses count), by `estimatedTaxCharges` (date of taxes in the Portfolio's «Impatto imposte», "Tax impact") and by the texts of «Quest'anno» ("This year") and «Prima di vendere» ("Before selling"). The numbers differ only if within the same year a plusvalenza (capital gain) precedes a capital loss, which is why it looks inert. Analitiche and summary emails do not use it.
- **Decision**: removed the section at the top right; the choice remains as a compact row at the bottom of the tab (with the caveat on estimates and an «i» explaining the two regimes), because IBKR and DEGIRO are normally under the declarative regime. `tax_regime` column, API and Umami event unchanged.
