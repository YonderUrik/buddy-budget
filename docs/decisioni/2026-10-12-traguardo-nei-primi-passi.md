# «Fissa il tuo traguardo» ("Set your goal") in Primi passi ("First steps") (2026-10-12)

The fourth step of Primi passi («Imposta un obiettivo», "Set a goal", a generic link to Analitiche) did not say what to do: you landed among sliders and answers without a field to fill in. The intention was to have the user save at least one FIRE plan assumption (or a budget), and the assumption that really matters is the annual spending to cover, from which the FIRE number and the arrival year follow.
Decision: keep the step and make it clear instead of removing it. It is now called «Fissa il tuo traguardo» and leads to `/analitiche?traguardo=1`, which opens the «Il tuo traguardo» ("Your goal") sheet: a single figure (how much you need in a year, prefilled from transactions), with the net worth that is enough and the year you get there computed while typing. If the figure stays the one from transactions it is saved as «dai miei dati» ("from my data": it updates itself). Whoever arrives from there does not see Analitiche's initial guide open on top.
The step still counts as done with a budget too, as before, so the checklist does not reappear for those who had completed it that way.
Observability: Umami events `analytics_goal_opened` and `analytics_goal_saved` (`source`: data/typed); saving goes through the existing route with the log `analytics.assumptions.saved`.
