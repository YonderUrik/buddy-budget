# 2026-10-14 — Landing «Sei domande» ("Six questions") and login in the same style

**Decision.** The buddybudget.io home is rebuilt from scratch as a scrolling story (direction «D rivista», "D revised", chosen by Daniele among six proposals): on the left six questions scroll by («Dove vanno i soldi ogni mese?», «Come vanno i miei investimenti?», «Quanto pago di tasse se vendo?», «Mi conviene estinguere il mutuo prima?», «Quando posso smettere di lavorare?», «E in tutto, quanto ho?» — "Where does the money go each month?", "How are my investments doing?", "How much tax do I pay if I sell?", "Should I pay off the mortgage early?", "When can I stop working?", "And altogether, how much do I have?"), on the right an app window stays fixed and answers. Where the screenshot alone does not tell the story (transactions ending up in the groups, value versus paid in, tax receipt, mortgage with and without extra, goal year that moves) the window shows «live» HTML panels, with «Vedi la schermata vera» ("See the real screen") to switch to the screenshot; Panoramica and the security page are real screenshots on which the camera zooms into the panel that matters. On the phone the window becomes a phone pinned at the top, with phone-sized versions (`<id>-phone.jpg`, new in the capture; the security page is captured by opening the link from the portfolio, `open` in `screens.ts`). Logos of banks (flags of the five countries), brokers and instruments sit right in the opening; no highlighted box while scrolling. Copy and figures in `landing/content/story.ts`, components in `landing/components/story/`.

Below the story: «Funziona con quello che hai già» ("Works with what you already have"), «La sicurezza, in chiaro» ("Security, plainly": PSD2 explained in a couple of words, what is encrypted, where the data live, what we do not save: only facts already verified in `content/security.ts`, never end-to-end encryption, disk encryption, audits or «neanche noi vediamo i dati», "not even we see the data"), «Il codice è aperto» ("The code is open": back on the home at Daniele's explicit request: it supersedes the choice of 2026-10-12 to remove it), the Italian rules, the calculators, six frequently asked questions (the long FAQs stay on `/sicurezza`), navy closing. Static navigation (no fixed bar), navy footer. New `/strumenti` hub and calculator `/strumenti/obiettivo-di-risparmio` (`lib/tools/obiettivo.ts`, tested). Removed from the home: spreadsheet in the opening, tabbed app tour, comparison, «per chi è» ("who it's for"), catalog (`/funzioni` remains). New Umami events: `story_step_viewed`, `story_real_toggled`; sections and CTA locations updated.

The app login follows the same style (`AuthFrame`, `AuthPreview`, `AuthFootLinks` in `components/domain/auth`): form panel on the left with links to the site (Sicurezza, Codice aperto, Aiuto), on the right the Panoramica in a window that builds up item by item to net worth, with the same figures as the landing. Removed the login spreadsheet (`AuthSheet`, formula bar, animated example) decided on 2026-10-11.

**Why.** The previous proposals had looked «fatte dall'AI» ("made by AI": gradient backgrounds, text-only boxes). The request was: tell what you see in the app with the real screens, animate only where a change explains better than an image, put the logos first, explain security without acronyms (it is the first question of anyone who has to link their bank) and show that the code is open; landing, login and app in a single style.

**Postponed.** Open Graph image; test on Safari/iPhone; pages per question; framings designed for the landing (today the camera works on the full screenshots); login preview on phone (hidden below `lg`); Lighthouse measurement of the new home.
