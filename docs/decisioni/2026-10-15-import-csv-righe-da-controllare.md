# Personal CSV import: rows to check, not «correggi il file» ("fix the file") (2026-10-15)

From a support report (iPhone, dark theme): the analysis of a CSV stopped at «Alcune righe richiedono verifica. Correggi il file o rigenera il formato» ("Some rows need checking. Fix the file or regenerate the format"), without saying which rows, why, or how to regenerate. The message originated in `lib/personal-import/worker.ts` when the parser (AI-generated or saved) returned at least one `error` outcome: the whole import was blocked, the parser was not saved and the per-row detail sat below the report, with only the first reason.
Now the «Righe da controllare» ("Rows to check") screen (`PersonalImportReview`) says how many rows out of how many, groups the errors by reason with the file's row number and original text, and offers three paths: **import the recognized rows skipping the others** (action `import_skipping_errors`: the skipped rows become excluded without a receipt, so once fixed and re-uploaded they are new again; the generated parser, which travels encrypted in the preview, becomes the format's parser), **re-analyse with the AI without re-uploading** (action `reanalyze`: the text is rebuilt from the preview rows, consent asked again, the old job disappears from the list), **upload a corrected file**. The job message, the list, the island and the email all say the same thing.
No migration (`skipped` is a field of the `importLedger` jsonb). Postponed: editing rows directly in the preview, richer error reasons from the parser (offending column), downloadable summary of the skipped rows.
