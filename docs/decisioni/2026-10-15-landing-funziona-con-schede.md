# 2026-10-15 — «Funziona con» ("Works with") in cards, with uniform logos and icons

**Decision.** The home's «Funziona con quello che hai già» ("Works with what you already have") section (`#con`) goes from a grey band to four cards with margins, border and corners (`--surface`, `--line`, `--r-card`), arranged in two columns that follow the content height (on phone one column, in text order). Each card has at the top a lucide icon in a tinted box (same scheme as the icons of «La sicurezza, in chiaro») and at the bottom: banks with flag and country name; brokers as tiles all of the same height on a light background even in dark theme (the marks are designed for light), with «CSV di chiunque» ("Anyone's CSV") as a dashed full-width tile; instruments as chips with the price source (Yahoo Finance, «Tra le fonti dei prezzi», "Among the price sources") on a separate row; the rest with chips.

The instrument chips (shared with the home's opening) no longer have abbreviations in a small circle but an icon in a box of the instrument's colour: `InstrumentChip.code` becomes `icon` (names added to `HomeIcon`: candles, layers, bitcoin, car, spreadsheet) and the `navy` colour, invisible in dark theme, becomes `ink`. The light plates behind logos in dark theme remain only in the opening (`.with .logo`); in the cards each tile has its own.

**Real logos from Logo.dev (at Daniele's request).** Enel, VWCE (Vanguard logo, the issuer, as the app does for ETFs), BTP (logo of the MEF, the Italian Ministry of Economy and Finance, which issues the bonds) and Bitcoin show the real logo instead of the icon; Fondo pensione, Mutuo and Prestito auto (pension fund, mortgage, car loan), which are concepts and not brands, keep the icon. The files are downloaded once with `landing/scripts/fetch-market-logos.mjs` (public key `LOGODEV_PUBLISHABLE_KEY`, WebP 128 px) and served from `public/logos/market/`: no requests to Logo.dev from the visitor's browser, in line with the choice of 2026-10-05, and no key in the site. The credit «Loghi forniti da Logo.dev» ("Logos provided by Logo.dev") sits in the footer of every page (condition of the free plan); provenance is in `public/logos/ATTRIBUTION.md`. To be clarified with support@logo.dev (already noted on 2026-10-05): commercial use of the free plan and issuers' trademarks (Vanguard), which in the app keep the abbreviation until use is authorized.

**Why.** After #371 was merged Daniele reported that the grey box had no margins and was square-cornered (it was the global `.band` class, with `--bg-alt` background, applied without padding or radius) and that the provider and security icons were ugly (logos of different heights, tiny Fineco, small circles with two letters).

**Postponed.** Nothing new (logos for pension fund and loans: there is no generic brand); the items of the 2026-10-14 decision remain (Open Graph, Safari/iPhone, pages per question).
