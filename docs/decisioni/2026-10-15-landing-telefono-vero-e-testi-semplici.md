# 2026-10-15 — Landing: a real phone, whole screenshots and plain texts

**Decision.** On the landing home page the phone pinned at the top on mobile is no longer a navy rectangle but an iPhone-like frame made in CSS (`landing/components/phone-frame.tsx` + `.css`: body with a glossy edge, black bezel, island, status bar with time and icons, side keys, home indicator; new `--phone-*` tokens in `globals.css`, no colours in components). The screen has 390×844 proportions and leaves 390×751 to the app, i.e. an iPhone screen minus the status bar and the home-indicator band: the `<id>-phone.jpg` captures are taken at that size (`PHONE` in `capture-screens.mjs`), so the screen fits whole, with no cropping. `PhoneShot` puts a capture inside the frame; `PhoneFrame` also hosts the live panels of the story.

**Screenshots never cropped.** The app window in the story (`story-stage.tsx`) no longer zooms (1.25 → 1): every screenshot is shown at full width, in a 4:3 window as tall as the viewport allows, with a "camera" that only scrolls vertically to frame the point of the question (80 px margin above, never past the image edges). The zoom cut off sidebars and cards: that was the defect Daniele reported.

**More screenshots.** New section «Com'è fatta, schermata per schermata» ("How it looks, screen by screen", `#dentro`, `components/tail/screens-home.tsx`) right after the story: it reuses the `/funzioni` gallery (`ScreenGallery`, with `phoneOnMobile`) with all 17 screens of the catalog, whole; on the phone it shows the phone version inside the frame. For this every screen in `content/screens.ts` now has `phone: true`, and `PHONE_ONLY=1` in the capture script regenerates only those versions (guide in `docs/landing-screens.md`). No extra library: gsap was already on the home page.

**Plain texts.** All home texts (`content/story.ts`), the FAQ (`site.ts`), the Italian rules (`home.ts`) and the calculators (`tools.ts`) are rewritten in everyday words: no «plusvalenze», «minusvalenze», «TWR», «quote», «ipotesi»; we say «guadagno» (gain), «perdite da scalare» (losses to offset), «quanto hai pagato» (what you paid), «quando puoi smettere di lavorare» (when you can stop working). Second pass on 2026-10-16 at Daniele's request: one idea per sentence, no lists or parentheses inside a sentence, 41 texts from 1,108 to 944 words. The names of tax concepts stay in the calculators where they are needed (zainetto fiscale, the Italian loss carry-forward), explained in a sentence. The encryption sentence in «La sicurezza, in chiaro» stays, because `security.test.ts` ties it to `FIELD_ENCRYPTION_LIVE`.

**Why.** Daniele's request on the evening of 2026-10-15: he did not like the phone mock-up, the texts were too hard, he wanted more screenshots and some looked cropped.

**Deferred.** Open Graph image, Safari/iPhone check (the frame is pure CSS, but it must be seen live), per-question pages, login preview on the phone (already in `#bb-backlog`); gallery captions with the "why" of each screen.
