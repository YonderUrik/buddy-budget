# Savings goals inside Analitiche (Analytics) (2026-10-15)

Daniele chose proposal D (`/mnt/project-files/obiettivi-risparmio/`): **no new sidebar item**, goals are an «Obiettivi» ("Goals") view of Analitiche (`/analitiche/obiettivi`, next to «Risposte», "Answers"), a mix of piggy banks (A) and a single plan (B), and they are not «watertight compartments»: a goal is funded with portions of accounts and investment positions.
The month's savings (the same as in Analitiche, `loadAnalyticsSnapshot`, shared with the sidebar FIRE module) go to the goals **in order of priority**, each up to the chosen share; from there the arrival date (without growth), status and how much each goal **moves the FIRE target** (the emergency fund does not move it: the money stays). Sources are **labels**, they do not move money; securities count net of the estimated tax if sold today. Tables `goals` and `goal_sources` (encrypted name), migration 0038, which also adds `goal_alerts_enabled` to the preferences.
Opt-in email reminders (Impostazioni → Notifiche or from the last step of the form): goal reached, date 30 days away without being on track, and at the start of the month (by the 3rd) where you stand with a prompt to update the money set aside; «Obiettivi» row in the periodic summary. «Novità» ("What's new") in Panoramica and «I tuoi obiettivi» ("Your goals") section (dismissible prompt until there is one), entry in the catalog and in the landing story. Disabled for Germany like the rest of Analitiche.
Postponed (phases 2-3 of the README): pension and debts as sources or goals (payoff), debt instalments in the capacity, «after» effects (e.g. hypothetical mortgage), path chart, drag to reorder (today arrows), automatic history of the money set aside.
