# Phone bottom bar as a floating capsule (2026-10-17)

The bottom bar under 768 px is no longer a full-width strip glued to the edge: it is a rounded capsule inset 12 px from the sides and from the bottom (plus the safe area), translucent with blur, like the iOS 26 tab bar and Material 3's taller bar. Icons are 24 px; the active tab has a light grey pill (dark grey in the dark theme) with icon and label in the brand teal. Daniele rejected the yellow pill used for one round inside the native app PR #404. `BOTTOM_NAV_HEIGHT_REM` now includes the gaps, so the content spacer and `--bottom-nav-offset` (sync island, Categorizza) leave room for the whole capsule. Shipped on its own, off main, so the mobile web gets it before the native app; #404 drops its copy of the change. Landing phone screenshots regenerated (`PHONE_ONLY=1`).
