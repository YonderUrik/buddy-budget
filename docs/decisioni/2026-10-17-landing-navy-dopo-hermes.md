# 2026-10-17 — Landing: navy opening and tall titles, after Hermes Agent

Daniele liked hermes-agent.nousresearch.com and asked to take inspiration from it in our own language. Taken: one colour as the world (our navy for the bar, the opening, the closing and the footer), tall light condensed titles (Sofia Sans Extra Condensed, free, as `--f-d`), small numbered labels in the mono face («#1 · Da dove»), the product shown big right after the opening (the Panoramica, full width, half on the navy), the install commands to copy («Sotto il cofano», from `docs/self-hosting.md`, Umami `self_host_command_copied`) and the name drawn across the footer.
Left out: their engraved and halftone illustrations (only real screenshots here), the electric blue and the blurred frame (no gradients or glow), capitals everywhere (hard to read in long text on phones).
Second round, same day, on Daniele's notes: the brand yellow `--sun` is the main button everywhere («Inizia gratis», the closing, the phone bar); every content and legal page opens on a navy head (back link, yellow kicker, tall title, lead); lines that told nothing were removed (the trust list and «gratis durante la beta», «ogni mattina», section intros and kickers, the «#1 ·» labels on the sections). The sign-in pages use the same tall title, the navy preview and a yellow main button. In the app (scope «Titoli e giallo», chosen by Daniele): page titles in the display face (`PAGE_TITLE` in `components/domain/shared`), the active sidebar item marked by a yellow bar and the bottom bar by a yellow pill, the page's main action as the new `sun` button variant; the sidebar stays light. Landing screenshots regenerated. Rules in `landing/DESIGN.md`.
