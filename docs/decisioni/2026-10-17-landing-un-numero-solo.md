# 2026-10-17 — Landing «Un numero solo»: home rebuilt around one promise, inner pages and sign-in aligned

**Decision.** The buddybudget.io home is rebuilt from scratch around a single promise, «Tutti i tuoi soldi, in un numero solo» (direction A, chosen by Daniele among three directions presented on a decision page: A «il numero vero», B «prima di vendere» for investors, C «le spese in ordine da sole»). The page is the Panoramica unrolled, in six blocks: the opening (title, one sentence, «Inizia gratis», four trust facts, the real Panoramica in a window with a **sum card** pulled out of its corner: bank accounts + investments + pension fund − loans = net worth, the rows appearing one at a time and the total counting up once); «Da quattro posti, un numero» (how each place gets in, as four ledger rows with flags and broker logos, closed by the «=» line); «Cosa ti dice, ogni giorno» (five tabs like the app's segmented control: spese, investimenti, tasse, mutuo, futuro, each with the real screen, the figure from that screenshot and a hook; the panels sit in a native scroll-snap track, so on the phone they swipe); «Legge soltanto. Non sposta soldi.» (four verified facts with sources, two limits said plainly) with the navy **«Sotto il cofano»** panel for tinkerers (AGPL-3.0, stack, Docker Compose, PSD2, encryption, export, SECURITY.md, GitHub and self-hosting links when the repository is public); seven questions as rows that open on tap (plus «Posso installarlo su un server mio?» when the repository is public); navy closing. Copy in `landing/content/home.ts`, components in `landing/components/home/`.

**Two registers, one page.** Every section speaks to non-experts first (headline and one sentence in everyday words, no «plusvalenze», «PSD2» or percentages in titles) and gives the technical fact right after, smaller: the `spec` line under each of the four places, the sources under the trust facts, the whole «Sotto il cofano» panel. Titles are 5–7 words.

**Mobile first.** The links fold into a full-screen sheet with 58 px rows and the two buttons; a fixed bottom bar carries «Inizia gratis» from the end of the opening to the closing; the five panels swipe; the questions are 44 px rows; calculator fields are 44 px tall. No scroll-jacking: the page scrolls like a page.

**Everything in one system.** `ContentPage` (calculators, guide, features, security, screens, 404) ends with the same navy `Closing` as the home instead of its own card, its title scale and kicker pill match the home, the `/sicurezza` eyebrows are gone. The app's sign-in preview (`AuthPreview`) shows the same four places with the same rounded figures as the home's sum card and the caption «Tutti i tuoi soldi, in un numero solo.» (catalog entries added in en/de; the «6 di 6» ones removed).

**Removed from the home** (consciously, after four iterations): the pinned scrolling story with the six questions and its live panels (`components/story`, `content/story.ts`), the full gallery (stays at `/schermate`), «Funziona con» as a section (flags and logos now sit in the four rows), the Italian rules as a section (one sentence in the Tasse tab; `ITALY` stays for `llms.txt`), the calculators section (the Tasse and Mutuo tabs link their calculator; `/strumenti` stays in the menu), the instrument chips and their Logo.dev logos (`public/logos/market`, `fetch-market-logos.mjs` and the footer credit, no longer needed). Umami: `SectionId` is now `come | dentro | sicurezza | codice | domande | fine | schermate`, `story_*` events replaced by `showcase_tab_selected`, `cta_click.location` gains `mobile_bar` and `dentro`, `target` gains `how`.

**Why.** After the «Sei domande» home and its two touch-ups Daniele still was not convinced: the home kept carrying earlier points, and the texts explained instead of saying. Research on landing structure (one outcome in the headline, one action, trust next to the button, the product shown early) and the copywriting skills (positioning first, headline = brand + promise, clarity over cleverness) were applied, with a decision page of three directions and a copy pass to five-to-seven-word titles.

**Also fixed.** In the light theme the window and phone frames showed the dark screenshot (a `display: block` rule beat the theme switch of `app-shot.css`; the gallery's phone frame had the same bug), and `/schermate` now shows the phone-size captures on the phone. `og.png` regenerated with the new promise on the navy ground (`scripts/make-og-image.mjs`).

**Deferred.** Safari/iPhone check of the sheet, the bottom bar and the swipe track; `PRODUCT.md` for the design skill (product truth lives in `CLAUDE.md`); a Lighthouse pass on the new home; the sign-in preview on the phone (hidden below `lg`, as before); a check of the OG image on the social networks.
