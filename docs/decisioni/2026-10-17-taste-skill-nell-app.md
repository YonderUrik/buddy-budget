# 2026-10-17 — taste-skill in the app: headings and quiet lists

- **Question**: Daniele asked how every section of the app would change if taste-skill were applied as fully as on the landing, and whether it makes sense. The skill itself says it is "not for dashboards or app screens"; PR #417 had already applied its house rules to the app (two faces, no gradients, no resting shadows, filled cards).
- **Dropped**: each screen opening on a navy colour field with its key figure at poster size. Daniele did not want the background to change inside a page. Also dropped: condensed titles in the sidebar modules (they outweighed the figures in the narrow column; five variants compared, he kept today's sidebar untouched).
- **Applied (approved 2026-10-18)**: section headings without the tinted icon, in the condensed face (`SECTION_TITLE`, 2.25rem) and the same everywhere, including «Dove sta il patrimonio», Impostazioni groups and sections and Aiuto sections (no icon tiles; on the phone each Impostazioni group is a condensed title with its line); no thin rules between rows and sections inside pages (real tables, dialogs, form fields and sticky action bars keep theirs); no small capitals for dates in pages; buttons and segmented controls are pills, outline buttons filled; no icons in the section tabs and the Analitiche answer labels; no small section name above the view title on the phone.
- **Not applied, on purpose**: the floating capsule bottom bar with blur (Daniele's choice in #413), the motion layer (against the performance standard), pictures, violet among the user's category colours, icons in navigation and in category chips (they help recognise the item).
- **Rule for new screens**: section headings use `SECTION_TITLE` with no icon; separate list rows with space, not `divide-y`/`border-b`, unless it is a real table.
