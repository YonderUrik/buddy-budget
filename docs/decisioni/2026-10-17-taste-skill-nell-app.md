# 2026-10-17 — taste-skill applied to the app screens (prototype, awaiting Daniele's choice)

- **Question**: Daniele asked how every section of the app would change if taste-skill were applied as fully as on the landing, and whether it makes sense. The skill itself says it is "not for dashboards or app screens"; PR #417 had already applied its house rules to the app (two faces, no gradients, no resting shadows, filled cards).
- **Prototype** (draft PR, nothing ships until chosen): each screen opens on a navy colour field (`HeroField`, dark tokens tinted towards navy, edge to edge) with its key figure in the display face at poster size; section headings without the tinted icon and one size larger; no thin rules between rows (CSS layer in `globals.css`, real tables and form fields keep theirs); no tiny spaced capitals; every button and segmented control a pill, outline buttons filled; no icons in the section tabs and in the Analitiche answer labels; no small section name above the view title on the phone.
- **Not applied, on purpose**: the floating capsule bottom bar with blur (the skill bans glass and floating pill navigation, Daniele chose it in #413), the motion layer (at least eight moving elements, against the performance standard: no animation on first appearance of data), real pictures (an app has no place for them), violet among the user's category colours.
