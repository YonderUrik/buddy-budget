Navy + yellow, the display titles and the real screenshots stay.

- **App** (the skill says it is not for app screens, so only its house rules): two faces (`font-heading` is Hanken with `tabular-nums`), no gradients (charts use flat fills), no shadows on resting elements, sentence-case labels instead of tiny capitals, cards filled instead of outlined, no glass on sticky bars. Floating layers (popovers, dialogs, islands, bottom bar) keep their shadow; row dividers in data lists stay.
