# Language and features from the visitor's country (2026-10-19)

**Decision**: the visitor's country (Cloudflare's `CF-IPCountry`) decides the language a newcomer lands in: Italy, San Marino and the Vatican Italian; Germany, Austria and Liechtenstein German (Switzerland German or Italian following the browser); everywhere else English, whatever the browser says. It applies to the sign-in page and onboarding (`proposedProfile`), the sign-in email of a new address, the public demo and the landing. A language picked by hand always wins and is kept (`bb-language` cookie in the app, `bb-landing-language` on the landing, `?lang=` from the landing to /login and /demo); without the header (self-hosted, local) the browser language decides, as before.
**New product country `OTHER`**: every country whose tax and pension rules the app does not model. It gets everything that doesn't depend on local rules; taxes, pension and dividend forecasts are blocked by the same route policy as Germany (`isCountryPathAvailable`). Analitiche, goals and the FIRE module stay open (Daniele, 2026-10-20: they run on the user's own figures), without capital gains tax or stamp duty. Proposed at sign-up from the detected country, changeable in Impostazioni («Altro paese»), never changed by travelling. No migration (the column is text). A fifth demo account, `OTHER`/`en`, is what a US visitor opens.
**Disabled, not hidden**: in the app Pensione (and, in Germany, Analitiche) stay in the menu dimmed with «Presto» and open `/country-unavailable` («Non ancora disponibile nel tuo paese. Arriverà presto.»); the tap sends `country_feature_requested` to Umami, to count demand. `NavLock` is generic so the subscription gate can lock items the same way; the country lock comes first. On the landing pension and tax blocks are hidden for countries without those rules (pension only for Germany), with one «coming soon» line where a block disappears; crawlers get no country cookie and see the full page.
**Why**: a visitor from the US saw an Italian app and Italian tax and pension features that cannot work for them.
**Open**: `OTHER` does not remember which country it was; Cloudflare's IP geolocation must stay on (check once in production); Cookie page updated without a new legal version (informative only).
