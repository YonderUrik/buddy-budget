# Language and features from the visitor's country (2026-10-19)

**Decision**: the visitor's country (Cloudflare's `CF-IPCountry`) decides the language a newcomer lands in: Italy, San Marino and the Vatican Italian; Germany, Austria and Liechtenstein German (Switzerland German or Italian following the browser); everywhere else English, whatever the browser says. It applies to the sign-in page and onboarding (`proposedProfile`), the sign-in email of a new address, the public demo and the landing. A language picked by hand always wins and is kept (`bb-language` cookie in the app, `bb-landing-language` on the landing, `?lang=` from the landing to /login and /demo); without the header (self-hosted, local) the browser language decides, as before.
**New product country `OTHER`**: every country whose tax and pension rules the app does not model. It gets everything that doesn't depend on local rules; only the pension and the investment taxes tab are closed (`isCountryPathAvailable`). Daniele, 2026-10-20 and 2026-10-21: everything else stays visible, so income, Analitiche, goals and FIRE run on the user's own figures before tax (no capital gains tax, withholding or stamp duty). Germany now gets Analitiche too, the same way; only its pension stays closed. Proposed at sign-up from the detected country, changeable in Impostazioni («Altro paese»), never changed by travelling. No migration (the column is text). A fifth demo account, `OTHER`/`en`, is what a US visitor opens.
**Disabled, not hidden**: in the app Pensione stays in the menu dimmed with «Presto» and open `/country-unavailable` («Non ancora disponibile nel tuo paese. Arriverà presto.»); the tap sends `country_feature_requested` to Umami, to count demand. `NavLock` is generic so the subscription gate can lock items the same way; the country lock comes first. On the landing pension blocks are hidden outside Italy and tax blocks outside Italy and Germany, with one «coming soon» line where a block disappears; crawlers get no country cookie and see the full page.
**Why**: a visitor from the US saw an Italian app and Italian tax and pension features that cannot work for them.
**Open**: `OTHER` does not remember which country it was; Cloudflare's IP geolocation must stay on (check once in production); Cookie page updated without a new legal version (informative only).
