# «Cerca»: one search for the whole app, sentences included (2026-10-25)

Daniele asked for an Apple Spotlight-style search over everything in the app, where a typed sentence can also add something, with care for abuse and for sentences in other languages. He chose the grey («Neutra») selection, pulling the page down on the phone (with a «Cerca» field in «Altro» as a fallback) and, on desktop, a field at the right of the page title, plus ⌘K / Ctrl K and «/».

- One panel finds movements, accounts, securities, categories, subscriptions, debts, goals, the pension fund, pages, settings and «Aggiungi» actions. Pages, settings and actions are matched in the browser; the user's data by `GET /api/search`, a few small queries per group. Movement descriptions are encrypted, so they are decrypted and matched for the last 24 months only, at most 6,000 movements per search; older ones are found with the text search in Movimenti over a longer period.
- A result lands where it says: one movement opens its detail over the current page, the same merchant many times, an account or a category open Movimenti filtered (`?testo=`, `?conto=`, `?categoria=`).
- A sentence («pagato 12 € al bar ieri», «sold 10 VWCE at 110», «gestern 12 € bei Rewe bezahlt») is read by rules in the browser, in Italian, English and German whatever the app language, and opens the «Aggiungi» form already filled in; nothing is saved without the user's tap. Questions stay searches.
- What the rules cannot read can go to the AI only on an explicit tap («Chiedi all'AI»): only the sentence, today's date and the language, through OpenRouter with zero data retention; 10 questions per hour and 40 per day per person, 3,000 per day for the service, a 24-hour cache, fail closed when Redis is down, never in the public demo. It reuses the AI consent, renamed «Assistente AI» and now covering «Cerca» too; Privacy, Terms and Cookie texts updated (`LEGAL_VERSION` 2026-10-25, everyone re-accepts).
- Open choices proposed by Claude and taken while Daniele had not answered: no full-history text scan, no «Cerca … sul mercato» row yet (a Yahoo call on tap, later), «Chiedi all'AI» for Pro only once paid plans exist (#466).

Rejected: a yellow selection (too loud), the field in the sidebar, AI on every keystroke (cost and privacy), a separate consent for «Cerca».
