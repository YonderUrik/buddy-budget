# 2026-11-03 — «Percorso»: the path of the money in Analisi

Daniele asked for a Sankey diagram in Liquidità › Analisi: income splitting into needs and wants, then into categories. Three proposals were tried in the real app with the demo data (a full left-to-right Sankey, a two-column view that opens one group at a time, a top-to-bottom cascade with a list). He chose the full Sankey on desktop and the two-column steps on the phone, and asked for it to be something the user turns on, since it says much the same as «Dove vanno i soldi».

So «Dove vanno i soldi» gets a switch, «Per gruppi | Percorso». Groups (ring and list) stay the default; the choice is remembered per device (`usePersistedFlag`). In «Percorso» the section takes the full width on desktop. At 640 px of container width or more the picture is the four-column Sankey (sources → Entrate → groups and «Messo da parte» → categories, hover lights a node's bands); below it is the phone steps (Entrate → groups, a tap opens a group into all its categories). A tap on a category opens Movimenti filtered on it, as in the groups view. When spending exceeds income the gap enters as «Presi dai risparmi».

Built without a chart library (`lib/liquidity/money-flow.ts` builds nodes and links, `lib/liquidity/sankey-layout.ts` places them as SVG bands), since Recharts' Sankey cannot place labels outside the bands or open one group. Umami `liquidity_spend_view_changed` and `liquidity_flow_group_opened`; no logs or metrics (client-side picture of data already loaded). No migration, no new API. Catalog entry «Analisi e cash flow» and a «Novità» entry updated.
